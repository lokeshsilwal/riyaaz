    Settings

    Your Sa (scale)

    Many men pitch Sa between C and D in octave 3; many women between G# and B in octave 3. Use fine tune to match a harmonium or a recording.

    Tuning of the swaras

    Natural tuning matches the tanpura: Ga, Dha and Ni sit a little lower than on a harmonium.

    Tanpura

    Auto picks Pa, or Ma for raags without Pa (such as Malkauns). Earphones work best: the app can tell the tanpura from your voice when the tanpura plays through the speaker, but your singing should be clearly louder than it.

    Practice

    Microphone

    Off by default so your voice is not altered. Everything stays on this phone — nothing is recorded or uploaded.

    Raag

    Notation: Re komal (flat) · Ma tivra (sharp) · Ni lower octave (mandra) · Sa upper octave (taar)
